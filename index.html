There are cities where you make your fortune. And then there's Vice City — where fortune means nothing unless everybody knows you have it.

Under the palm trees and neon lights, every mansion is a competition, every sports car is an announcement, and every diamond tells a story.

Enter KOWALSKI. A fictional jewel of Leonida's endless appetite for luxury. A glittering parody of a world where even the peacocks dress for attention.

No promises of riches. Just the absurdity of wanting the crown.
